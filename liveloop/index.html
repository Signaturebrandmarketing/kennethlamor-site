How it works

Three steps. Then it runs every week.

LiveLoop is the third step of Signature DNA™: Scale. Your story and your image go in once, and your brand shows up every week without running your life.

  1. 01

    We learn you

    A Signature DNA™ interview captures your story, your voice and your topics. From it we build your Brand Kit and your first collection of editorial-style images.

  2. 02

    You approve

    Your whole week arrives drafted: posts in your brand voice and story videos starring you. Approve it all in one tap, edit a post, or pass.

  3. 03

    We publish everywhere

    On schedule, to every account you connect. Every post comes with a note on why it’s there, and every week LiveLoop learns what your audience responds to.

Every month

Why I built it

The strategy was right. The follow-through was the problem.

For years I handed clients beautiful images and a solid plan, and then real life showed up. No time, no team, no budget to keep it going. The images sat in a folder.

So I stopped writing plans that run on willpower and built the system that keeps going. I run my own brand on it. The magazine cover on this page is from my own November Issue in LiveLoop.

AI can copy your handwriting. It can’t mimic your signature.
